## High-for-public items — narrowed, still need Carl's confirmation

- **08 F1 — implemented rate limits don't match §9.** NARROWED, not refuted.
  The recipient-side gate already enforces an aggregate inbound ceiling, a
  per-sender ceiling and a per-day ceiling (`src/knok/gate.py:194-213`
  `_within_limits`: scopes `("messages","all")`, `("sender",sender)`,
  `("messages","all-day")`), and the probation contact-request limit lives in
  `src/knok/contacts.py:320` (daily, inbound overall, not per peer).
  `src/knok/policy.py:22` explicitly says the recipient-side names are NOT the
  spec's hub-side limits (#119): "the spec's per-sender-across-recipients and
  hub-wide limits are hub-side controls, not these names". So the open question
  is hub-side only: does the hub enforce a per-sender aggregate across
  recipients, the 10,000/hour hub-wide ceiling, and the 5/hour low-engagement
  throttle? The review may have read the gate as the hub. Confirm against
  `src/knok/hub.py` (no probation/per-sender hits found there by grep) before
  filing.
- **08 F3 — approval keyed to peer ID, not the current key.** PARTLY ADDRESSED,
  enforcement still missing. The audit side exists and predates the review:
  `db5a858` (2026-11-20, #65/#86) records `presented_fingerprint` vs
  `decided_fingerprint` — "different means the peer rotated while the request
  was held, and the decision belongs to the key that was on screen". BUT the
  admission path does not enforce it: `contacts._state_locked` / `wire_state`
  key purely on the peer row's state, and the gate admits on
  `contacts.state(sender) == "accepted"`. So a rotation while held is recorded,
  not blocked. Needs Carl's call: is audit-only intended, or should approval
  require refusal + fresh request per SPEC §9?
